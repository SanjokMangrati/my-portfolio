# Lineage

Terrace mixes five personal sites from the Refero Styles library (styles.refero.design). About 1,340 styles were indexed, 40 personal portfolios were shortlisted from them, and these five were kept. Each one gave one idea; none of their colors, fonts or marks were copied.

| Reference | What it does well | What Terrace takes | What Terrace leaves |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Henry (henry.codes)](https://styles.refero.design/style/ff4b9eff-dc0b-4886-bd65-c2f5e9069318) | A developer's broadside: the headline is the hero, with a coordinates and local-time line under it. | The name as an enormous condensed headline, and the metadata strip (here with Darjeeling's coordinates, elevation and IST). | The cream paper and the gothic serif. |
| [Haley Park (haleys.design)](https://styles.refero.design/style/c47654a9-7d7a-4b2c-8e0a-cd9296719c69) | One deep forest-green mood, line art as structure, thin rules ending in crosses. | A single deep-green world (`estate`), structural line art (tea terraces instead of gothic arches), and rules with terminal crosses. | Whole-page dark green and the weight-200 serif, which are hard for a recruiter to scan. |
| [Alejandro Mejias (alejandromejias.com.au)](https://styles.refero.design/style/5f802b93-5f46-470f-baaf-173737189c9d) | Mono labels everywhere, a three-zone top bar with a status pill and live time, one accent used only for status and contact. | Bracketed mono labels, the three-zone TopBar, and an accent (`muscatel`) reserved for state and action. | The acid-lime accent and the 3D mascot. |
| [Maciej Zadykowicz (maciej.co)](https://styles.refero.design/style/f1b76a42-050e-4c9e-96e3-a77fbd718c68) | Projects as dense, readable rows with year, description and roles; the only color means "Open". | The project Ledger and semantic-only status color. | The multicolor mural and rounded dark cards. |
| [Daniël van der Winden (daniel.pizza)](https://styles.refero.design/style/e8eda526-d686-4e45-a60d-61b6503a8eda) | A calm left rail and a generous serif reading column that make a long bio easy to read. | The rail plus reading-column layout for About and case studies, and a single dark primary button. | The near-colorless palette. |

Considered and set aside: Shelby Kay (botanical olive, close in spirit but its edge-bleeding name hurts scanning), Dennis Snellenberg (portrait-led hero), Daniel Sun (poster type with gradients), Flowen (loud collage), Chester (digital garden, more blog than portfolio).

## The local layer

What none of the references have, and what makes Terrace specific to Sanjok:
- **Place as data.** Darjeeling's coordinates (`27°02′ N 88°16′ E`), elevation (`2,042 m`) and the live IST clock are content, not decoration.
- **Tea-estate material.** Terrace lines, stencil marks (`SM`, the seal), manifest-style skill lists with dotted leaders, ledger rows.
- **Local color names.** `estate` (tea-garden green), `muscatel` (second-flush amber), `train` (the blue toy-train engines), `leaf`, `mist`.
- **Type with local provenance.** Martel comes from the Indian Type Foundry and carries Devanagari, so a Nepali line can be added later without changing the system.
